Frequently Asked

What is CNP TV?

CNP TV (Cricket News Point) at matchcentral.in is a free live sports destination that brings real-time cricket and football scores, ball-by-ball updates, fixtures, streaming links, match highlights and the latest sports news into one fast, clean page — available 24×7 on mobile, tablet and desktop.

Is watching live matches on CNP TV free?

Yes. CNP TV (Cricket News Point) is completely free to use. There is no subscription, no login and no paid tier — open the page and start following live scores and matches straight away.

Which sports are covered on CNP TV?

Cricket (T20, ODI, Tests and domestic leagues), football (AFC, EPL, La Liga, Serie A, Champions League and international cups), plus world sports, badminton, kabaddi, tennis and more — with dedicated 24×7 sections for Willow Cricbuzz, FanCode, World Sports and Sony LIV events.

How often are the live scores updated?

Live scores refresh automatically every few minutes in the background, and the live ticker, live clock and running score on each match card update in real time so you always see the current state of play.

Can I use CNP TV on my mobile phone?

Yes. The layout is fully responsive and mobile-first with a compact bottom navigation bar, horizontal swipe carousels and touch-friendly controls. You can also add CNP TV to your home screen as a Progressive Web App.

Does CNP TV require an account or registration?

No registration is needed. CNP TV works without any account, and you can follow every live match instantly as a guest.
