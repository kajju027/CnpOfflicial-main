Key Players to Watch India: Shubman Gill, Jasprit Bumrah, Rishabh Pant, Yashasvi Jaiswal New Zealand: Tom Latham, Daryl Mitchell, Matt Henry, Kyle Jamieson Match Preview India begin their two-Test series against New Zealand at a venue where they have won just one of eight Tests, a win dating back to 1968. Shubman Gill's side are fourth on the World Test Championship table and need wins to keep their final hopes alive, but they face a New Zealand team that sits top of the standings and has won its last three Tests against India, including a 3-0 sweep in India in 2024 and a 2-0 home series win in 2019-20. The hosts have also just beaten England 2-1 in England. The Basin Reserve surface is expected to be a classic New Zealand seamer's deck, and India's decision to bowl first after winning the toss reflects their desire to exploit the early movement. Jasprit Bumrah returns to lead the attack after a lengthy absence, but his record against New Zealand is modest (45.44 average across five Tests). New Zealand's pace quartet of Matt Henry, Kyle Jamieson, Jacob Duffy and Nathan Smith will be licking their lips at the prospect of a green top, while their batting line-up, bolstered by the returns of Devon Conway, Rachin Ravindra and Tom Blundell, will look to post a competitive first-innings total and put pressure on India's middle order. Expert View / Prediction Experts lean towards New Zealand, given their home advantage, their recent dominance over India, and a surface tailor-made for their seam attack. India's best chance lies in Bumrah and the pace battery exploiting the new ball early and in Shubman Gill continuing his prolific form. However, the hosts' comfort in Wellington conditions and their psychological edge make them slight favourites. Prediction: New Zealand start as slight favourites.
