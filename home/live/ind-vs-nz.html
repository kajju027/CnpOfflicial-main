Weather is expected to be clear, but conditions and team news should be checked from official sources before publishing. Pitch & Conditions - Bay Oval is one of the better batting surfaces in New Zealand, offering true bounce and a quick outfield. - The average first-innings score across 11 full-length ODIs is 272, rising to 287 when associate matches are excluded; six of those 11 innings produced 280 or more, including three totals above 315. - The new ball still does plenty early, with 12 of 26 completed innings ending in an all-out result, and seven of those collapses coming inside 47 overs. - The toss has not been a decisive factor: teams batting first have won six of 13 ODIs, chasing sides seven. - India have a strong record here, winning by 90 runs and by seven wickets in January 2019; Kuldeep Yadav’s 4 for 45 in the first of those games remains the best return by an Indian bowler at the venue. - Weather in Mount Maunganui is forecast to be sunny and dry, a welcome change from the seam-friendly conditions in Auckland and Hamilton. The chance of rain is under 5% from toss until the closing stages, with a high of 23°C and a westerly wind of 14–18 km/h. A full 50-overs-per-side match looks the most likely outcome. Key Players to Watch India: Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Shreyas Iyer, Mohammed Siraj New Zealand: Finn Allen, Tim Seifert, Mitchell Santner, Matt Henry Match Preview India will look to seal the five-match series with a game to spare at a venue where they have enjoyed success in the past. The batting-friendly surface at Bay Oval should allow Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli to find form after scratchy outings in the series, while Shreyas Iyer will be eager to make an impression in his first ODI as captain. India’s seam attack, led by Mohammed Siraj and Arshdeep Singh, will test New Zealand’s top order, which has struggled badly against seam and bounce so far. New Zealand, on the other hand, are fighting to keep the series alive. Their batting has been the major concern, with the hosts failing to post competitive totals in either completed match. The return of Matt Henry strengthens the pace attack, and the clearer weather at Mount Maunganui should give the batters a better chance to express themselves. However, with key players missing and confidence low, the hosts face a steep challenge against an Indian side determined to close out the series. Expert View / Prediction Experts favour India to seal the series, given their 2–0 lead, the batting-friendly conditions at Bay Oval, and New Zealand’s ongoing struggles with the bat. The hosts’ best chance lies in exploiting the early movement with the new ball and putting pressure on India’s middle order, but India start as clear favourites. Prediction: India start as favourites.
